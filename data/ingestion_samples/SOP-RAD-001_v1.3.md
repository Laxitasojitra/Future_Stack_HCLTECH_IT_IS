# MRI and CT Scan Scheduling and Preparation (Version 1.3)

## 1. Purpose
This SOP describes how patients schedule and prepare for MRI and CT scans.

## 2. How to Schedule an MRI or CT Scan
2.1 An MRI or CT scan requires a doctor's order (radiology requisition) in the patient's hospital record. Self-referral is not accepted.
2.2 Once the order is in the record, the scan can be booked through the patient portal, the patient services assistant, or the Radiology front desk (Ext. 2210).
2.3 The patient must complete the MRI safety questionnaire at the time of booking (see Section 3).
2.4 For scans with contrast, a serum creatinine result from the last 30 days is required before the booking is confirmed.
2.5 Booking confirmation is sent by SMS with the date, time, location (Radiology, Ground Floor, Block A) and preparation instructions.
2.6 Scan slots are available Monday to Saturday, 6:00 AM to 11:00 PM, and Sunday, 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM, following the installation of a second MRI scanner.

## 3. MRI Safety Screening
3.1 Patients must declare any pacemaker, defibrillator, cochlear implant, metal implant, aneurysm clip, insulin pump, or metal fragments in the body.
3.2 Patients with a pacemaker or defibrillator require written clearance from Cardiology before an MRI is booked.
3.3 Patients who are or may be pregnant must inform the Radiology staff before the scan.

## 4. Preparation
4.1 MRI without contrast: no fasting is required.
4.2 MRI or CT with contrast: do not eat for 4 hours before the scan. Water is allowed.
4.3 Patients taking metformin who are scheduled for a contrast scan must follow the instructions given by their treating doctor.
4.4 Remove all jewellery, watches, hairpins and metal objects before an MRI.

## 5. On the Day of the Scan
5.1 Arrive 30 minutes before the scheduled time.
5.2 Bring a photo ID, the doctor's order (if printed), and any previous scan reports or CDs.

## 6. Reports
6.1 Routine MRI and CT reports are available within 24 hours on the patient portal.
6.2 Emergency scan reports are available to the treating doctor within 2 hours.

## 7. Rescheduling and Cancellation
7.1 Scans may be rescheduled or cancelled free of charge up to 12 hours before the slot.
7.2 Within 12 hours of the slot, contact the Radiology front desk (Ext. 2210).
